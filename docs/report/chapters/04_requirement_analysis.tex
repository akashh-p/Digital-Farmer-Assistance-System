\section{SDG Alignment}
The design, implementation, and deployment of the Digital Farmer Assistance System are fundamentally aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), ensuring the software delivers measurable global impact.

\subsection*{SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth}
The platform directly contributes to the economic empowerment of small-scale agricultural workers by dismantling information monopolies. By providing farmers with unmediated, direct access to daily market pricing trends, the system eradicates the reliance on predatory middlemen. This transparency significantly elevates the income potential of marginalized farmers, fostering an environment where agricultural labor equates to fair, decent economic growth and improved financial autonomy.

\subsection*{SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure}
The deployment of this system embodies the innovative utilization of modern digital infrastructure to modernize traditional, analog agricultural practices. By leveraging cloud computing, mobile technologies, and external data APIs, the system introduces cutting-edge industrial innovation to rural environments that are traditionally underserved by technological advances. It establishes a resilient digital connective tissue that enhances rural technological infrastructure, thereby fulfilling the core mandate of SDG 9.
