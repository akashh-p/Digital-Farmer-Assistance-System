\subsection{Functional Requirements}
Functional requirements represent the specific behavioral functions that the system must perform to satisfy the user's needs. The core capabilities of the Digital Farmer Assistance System are explicitly defined to track user interactions and data processing workflows.

FR1 & \textbf{User Management:} The system shall allow farmers to securely register, authenticate, and manage personalized profiles, including the selection of preferred crop types and geographic regions. \\
\addlinespace
FR2 & \textbf{Advisory Engine:} The system shall dynamically filter and provide crop-specific agricultural guidance based on the inputted growth stage, prevailing season, and the farmer's declared preferences. \\
\addlinespace
FR3 & \textbf{Information Retrieval:} The system shall aggregate and display daily updated market prices from various regional markets, alongside localized 5-day weather forecasts utilizing external API integrations. \\
\addlinespace
FR4 & \textbf{Alert System:} The system shall continuously monitor incoming meteorological data and push automated notifications or visual highlights on the dashboard for extreme weather warnings. \\
\addlinespace
FR5 & \textbf{Admin Control:} The system shall provide a comprehensive, secure dashboard for administrators to perform CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) operations on market prices, weather data, and advisory content. \\
\addlinespace
FR6 & \textbf{Interaction Module:} The system shall encompass a dedicated query resolution module allowing farmers to submit textual descriptions of agricultural issues to registered experts for tailored responses. \\
